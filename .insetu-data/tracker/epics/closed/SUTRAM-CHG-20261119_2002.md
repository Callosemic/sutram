## Description

* **Whole-Header Column Pan, Intent Position Bar, & 2-Column Viewport Clamp (`sutram/js/app_shell.js`):**
  - Styled `.position-bar-segment.on-screen` with each column's active tab intent (`--seg-intent`), enabled `touch-action: none` across `.position-bar` and `.col-header`, and applied `.is-focused` header tinting across all column capacities.
  - Clamped 2-column tablet viewport indices in `syncViewportCapacity()` to `Math.max(0, layoutRatios.length - maxCapacity)` and synchronized URL hashes during `_setViewportIndex()`.
  - Enabled horizontal column-pan swiping across the full `.col-header` surface (including tabs and buttons via `_isHeaderBarSurfaceEvent`) while preserving taps and vertical tab-HUD drags (`_activateDrag` aborts `_dotDragController`).
  - Added `refreshProjection()`, `@sutram:column-jump`, and `@sutram:force-refresh` routing on `.col-header`, and guarded `_handleSubTabClick()` force-refresh behind `wasFocusedCol`.
* **Two-Column `?chipRows` Dropdown & Column-Width Icon Rail (`yenvui/js/dropdown.js`, `yenvui/js/physics.js`, `sutram/js/app_shell.js`):**
  - Added `?chipRows` mode to `<yenvui-dropdown>` with `pinnedTop` sections above search, `sectionSlug` / `sectionIcon` / `sectionIntent` labels, `badgeIcon` column indicators, and `[data-compact-slugs]` collapse to a `30px` icon rail when anchored in columns `< 520px`.
  - Updated `anchorTopLayerPopover()` to measure `popoverEl.offsetWidth` and clamp horizontal viewport positioning.
* **4-Part Subtab Quick-Control Bar & `keepSelection` Bulk Lifecycle (`sutram/js/primitives.js`, `sutram/js/sutram_sdk.js`, `yenvui/js/async-btn.js`, `yenvui/js/dropdown.js`):**
  - Replaced the disabled empty state in `<sutram-subtab-menu>` with an always-interactive `slot="header"` quick bar (`[ Col 1 ] [ Col 2 ] [ Col 3 ] | ↻`) emitting `@sutram:column-jump` and `@sutram:force-refresh` with 900ms confirmation feedback.
  - Propagated `keepSelection` across `ExtensionRegistry.getAggregatableActions()`, `<yenvui-async-btn>`, `<yenvui-dropdown>`, `<sutram-variant-btn>`, and `<sutram-entity-actions>`, clearing `SelectionStore` automatically on settled bulk actions unless `keepSelection` is true.
  - Stopped `sutram:force-refresh` at the column header when the shell refreshes the view itself, so the host's window listener does not refresh it a second time.
  - Added `ExtensionRegistry.stopMetronome()` and `.unref?.()` on metronome timers in `sutram/js/sutram_sdk.js`.
* **Reentrant Body User-Select Lock & Early Horizontal Touch Claim (`yenvui/js/physics.js`):**
  - Moved `document.body.style.userSelect = 'none'` into `acquireBodyScrollLock()` / `releaseBodyScrollLock()` and claimed touches for horizontal-only pans (the header row) at `dx > dy && dx >= 3` in `YenvuiGestureController._handleTouchMove()`; cards and other both-axis hosts keep native scrolling until the axis locks.
* **Fitness & Behavioral Hardening (`tests/fitness/rules_sutram.py`, `tests/sutram_consumer_linter.py`, `tests/unit/test_sutram_registry.py`, `docs/`, `readme.md`, `CODE_INDEX.md`):**
  - Added `LEGACY_SHELL_HEADER_SLOT_BAN` to `tests/fitness/rules_sutram.py` and `keepSelection` to `ALLOWED_ENTITY_ACTION_KEYS` in `tests/sutram_consumer_linter.py`.
  - Expanded Node.js ESM behavioral assertions in `tests/unit/test_sutram_registry.py` and synchronized `ADR-0035`, `docs/yenvui_contracts.md`, `docs/01_engineering_standards.md`, `readme.md`, and `CODE_INDEX.md`.
