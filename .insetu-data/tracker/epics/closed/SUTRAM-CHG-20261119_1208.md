## Description

- **Unified Single-Row Column Headers & Viewport Position Bar (`sutram/js/app_shell.js`):** Replaced the 38px global launcher row and floating viewport dots pill with a 4px proportional `.position-bar` at the top of `.spatial-viewport` when `capacity < layoutRatios.length` and a unified 44px `.col-header` per column containing a left-pinned `<sutram-subtab-menu>`, a searchable active-tab switcher and category launcher `<sutram-dropdown>`, fitted inline open tabs with post-render `scrollWidth` clamping, and the right-aligned `.col-globals-rail` on the last visible column.
- **Adaptive Selection Tray & Post-Render Action Overflow Clamping (`sutram/js/primitives.js`):** Extracted `renderAdaptiveLabel`, added `[empty]` host collapse and Shadow-boundary `observeAssignedContainer` width adaptation (`>= 480px` vs `< 480px`) to `<sutram-selection-tray>`, added post-render `scrollWidth` overflow clamping to `<sutram-entity-actions>`, and updated `<sutram-subtab-menu>` to stay rendered and disabled in place when empty while hosting `extraContentNodes` view controls and deduplicating `menuStores` subscriptions.
- **Synchronous Store Waiters, Pre-Collapse Action Filters, & Homogeneous Bulk Variant Keys (`sutram/js/sutram_sdk.js`):** Added `StoreRegistry.onRegister` to replace `setInterval` polling in lazy `SutramContextMixin.subscribe`, added `unsubscribe` and `observeAssignedContainer` lifecycle helpers, added `ExtensionRegistry.setActionFilter` (evaluated prior to variant-set collapse on single and every-item bulk paths), added manifest readers (`getManifestEntries`, `collectManifestField`, `getExtensionNames`), and added `resolveSelectionEntityType`.
- **Chassis Dropdown, Input Range Attributes, & Post-Drag Click Suppression (`yenvui/js/dropdown.js`, `yenvui/js/inputs.js`, `yenvui/js/physics.js`):** Added section-preserving search filtering, checkable items, `onPointerDown`, and `trailingButton` actions to `<yenvui-dropdown>`, added `min`/`max` properties to `<yenvui-input>`, and added capture-phase post-drag synthetic `click` suppression to `YenvuiGestureController` with cross-controller clearing on stationary taps.
- **Fitness & Behavioral Hardening (`tests/fitness/rules_sutram.py`, `tests/unit/js_loader.mjs`, `tests/unit/test_sutram_registry.py`):** Enforced `SUTRAM_INLINE_STYLE_BAN` and `SUTRAM_UNDECLARED_PROPERTY` across `sutram/js/*.js`, tightened `METRONOME_MANDATE` on `sutram_sdk.js`, added capture-phase `window` event dispatch to `js_loader.mjs`, and expanded `test_sutram_registry.py` coverage.
